Intent becomes an outcome

Keep ordinary inventory useful when enhanced actions are unavailable.

Wallet and intent actions are capability-negotiated extensions to standard Native, MRECT and interstitial inventory. A slot that cannot execute the enhanced action still receives a normal renderable ad.

SDK capabilityDeclared from modules actually linked into the host application.
Publisher policyControls which actions are allowed for a placement.
Demand responseMay include an action only when the request and creative contract support it.
FallbackPreserves the ordinary creative click path instead of sitting out the auction.

Privacy and supply trust

Signals are evidence, not permission.

The optional trust module layers Play Integrity leases and hardware-backed request signatures onto server-side replay and publisher validation. It never turns a client heuristic into a guaranteed authenticity claim.
